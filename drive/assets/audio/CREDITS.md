## Engine

All from the pack *The Dyno Sessions: 190E* by **Chippy569**: a Mercedes-Benz 190E 2.3-16 (naturally aspirated 2.3 litre
inline four) held at fixed speeds on a dyno. Pack page: https://freesound.org/people/Chippy569/packs/9658/

"exh" files were recorded with an Audix D6 one foot behind the exhaust outlet, "bay" files with a DPA 4062 taped to the
radiator support under the bonnet. "on" is full engine load, "off" is no load or a closed throttle.

The number in each file name is the engine speed measured from the recording (firing frequency × 30), which is not
always what the original was labelled.

**Loops made from a sweep.** The pack has steady recordings only near 1000, 2500 and 6300 rpm, but it also has the
engine sweeping through the whole range. For each in-between speed, the stretch of the sweep within about 8 % of that
speed was resampled so the engine turns at a constant rate through it (the engine speed was tracked from the
recording), then cut into single engine cycles (two revolutions) which were strung together in shuffled order to make
a two-second loop. It is still the recorded engine at that speed; nothing is synthesised.
